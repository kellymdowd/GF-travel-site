Day 6 GF Tip
GF Travel Tip
Soy Sauce in Japan Contains Wheat
Nearly all soy sauce in Japan is brewed with wheat. It's in dipping sauces, marinades, soups, glazes — almost everything. Bring GF tamari packets from home. Most restaurants will let you use your own if you explain.
GF Gone Global
The thing most celiacs don't realize until they arrive in Japan: standard soy sauce contains wheat. It's in everything — sashimi dipping sauce, ramen broth, yakitori glaze, even rice seasonings sometimes. We brought GF tamari packets from home (the small single-serve ones). Most restaurants were happy for us to use our own once we showed the celiac dining card. This one tip changes the entire trip.

Day 13 GF Tip
GF Packing List
What to Pack for GF Travel in Japan
Celiac dining card in Japanese (most staff don't speak English). GF tamari packets — 6-8 small ones will last a two-week trip. GF snack bars for bullet trains. Translation app with camera mode for reading ingredient labels.
GF Gone Global
What I packed for Japan as a celiac: a laminated celiac dining card in Japanese (essential — most kitchen staff don't speak English), GF tamari packets (the small single-serve ones, 6-8 lasted the full trip), GF snack bars for bullet trains and long temple days, and a translation app with camera mode for reading ingredient labels in konbini. The card opened every conversation. Without it, Japan would have been much harder.
