GF Friendliness

Manageable

The Maldives is a manageable destination for celiac travelers, but success depends almost entirely on advance communication with your resort rather than on any broader national infrastructure — there's no national celiac association, and no meaningful grocery or restaurant access outside the resort you're staying at. The upside is that resort staff are well-practiced at accommodating international dietary requests: at Anantara Veli, all four on-site restaurants worked with me once I gave advance notice. Traditional Dhivehi cuisine's reliance on rice, fish, and coconut also means naturally GF dishes are easy to find once you're eating local.
