Gluten-Free Survival Guide

Key Phrases
"Je suis atteint(e) de la maladie cœliaque" I have celiac disease
"Est-ce que c'est sans gluten ?" Is this gluten-free?
"Est-ce que la sauce contient de la farine ?" Does the sauce contain flour?
"La contamination croisée" Cross-contamination
"Sans pain, s'il vous plaît" Without bread, please
"Ce n'est pas un choix, c'est une maladie" It’s a medical condition
Grocery Stores
  • Naturalia — best GF selection, organic focus, multiple locations
  • Monoprix — reliable GF aisle, widely available across Paris
  • Picard — frozen GF ready meals, pizzas, bread, and desserts
  • Bio c' Bon — organic supermarket with good GF range
Safe French Dishes
  • Buckwheat galettes — ask for 100% sarrasin (no wheat blend)
  • Steak frites — naturally GF, confirm frying oil is dedicated
  • Salade niçoise — tuna, eggs, olives, and vegetables, naturally GF
  • Macarons — almond-based, naturally GF (check per bakery)
  • French cheeses — nearly all naturally GF
  • Crème brûlée — classic dessert, naturally GF

Getting Around

Métro
16 lines covering the entire city. Trains run every 2–5 minutes. The fastest way to get almost anywhere in Paris.
€2.55 per ride · Navigo Easy card
RER
Regional express trains connecting Paris to suburbs and airports. RER C to Versailles, RER B to CDG airport.
Navigo Easy works within Paris zones
Vélib'
City bike-share with 1,400+ stations. E-bikes and classic bikes available. Great for scenic rides along the Seine.
€5/day pass · App required
Walking
Paris is surprisingly walkable. Most central neighborhoods are within 30 minutes of each other on foot.
Best way to discover Paris
Local Tip
Buy a Navigo Easy card at the airport — works on Métro, RER, and buses. €2 card + €2.55 per ride. Keep your ticket until you exit.

Travel Tips

  • "Maladie cœliaque" is taken more seriously than "sans gluten" — always lead with the medical term
  • Email restaurants 24–48 hours ahead explaining celiac needs (in French for best response)
  • Assemble GF picnics from Naturalia or Monoprix — Paris parks are perfect for it
  • All wine is naturally GF — enjoy freely :)
  • The GF scene appears to have improved dramatically since 2014 — dedicated bakeries now exist throughout the city
What I'd Do Differently
  • When I visited in 2014, GF options were very tricky — I basically lived off macarons! Today there are 9+ dedicated GF restaurants and bakeries that I wish I'd had access to. I guess I have to go back!
  • I'd take more time at Versailles — it's one of those places that surprised me even though I'm not usually a palace/museum person.
