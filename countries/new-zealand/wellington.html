Getting Around

Buses

Metlink runs Wellington's bus network. Snapper card (contactless) for cheaper fares. Most routes connect through the central hub at Lambton Quay.
Frequent services 6am–11pm

Cable Car

5-minute ride from Lambton Quay to the Kelburn lookout. $9 NZD return for adults. Connects to Botanic Garden and Zealandia shuttle.
Every 10 min, 7am–10pm

Walking

Wellington's CBD is compact and very walkable. Most restaurants, landmarks, and the waterfront are within 15 minutes on foot from the hotel area.
Best for the central city

Airport

Wellington Airport (WLG) is only 8 km from the CBD. Airport Express bus #91 runs every 10–20 minutes. Taxi or rideshare takes about 15 minutes.
~15 min to CBD
Pro Tip
Wellington is famously windy — hold onto hats and scarves on the waterfront. The wind is part of the charm, but it can catch you off guard, especially crossing the harbour bridge.

Frequently Asked Questions

Wellington scores 8/10 for GF friendliness (Easy). There are 8 restaurants with GF options, including a 100% dedicated GF Korean restaurant and a dedicated GF bakery. Staff awareness is generally high across the city.
MISS KANGSTA (5/5 GF score, 100% dedicated GF Korean); Half Baked Catering Co (4/5 GF score, dedicated GF bakery); Neo Cafe & Eatery (4/5 GF score, celiac owner).
New World and Countdown supermarkets in Wellington carry extensive free-from ranges. New Zealand has strong allergen labeling laws, making grocery shopping straightforward for celiacs.

Travel Tips

  • Wellington is NZ's food capital — don't skip Cuba Street for cafes, restaurants, and people-watching.
  • Contactless payment (tap-and-go) is accepted everywhere. Cash is rarely needed.
  • Book the Bluebridge ferry in advance for the South Island crossing — summer sailings fill up quickly.
  • The Weta Workshop in Miramar is a must for film fans — book the miniatures tour ahead of time.
  • New Zealand supermarkets have strong allergen labeling — GF products are clearly marked on shelf and packaging.
  • Uber works in Wellington, but the compact city means you'll rarely need it beyond airport transfers.
What I'd Do Differently
  • I'd make time for MISS KANGSTA — a 100% gluten-free Korean restaurant is a rare find anywhere in the world. Next time for sure.
  • I'd spend an extra day to visit Zealandia — the eco-sanctuary with native birds and tuatara is supposed to be magical at dawn.
