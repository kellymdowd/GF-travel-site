Getting Around

Bus
AT Metro network covers the entire city. No cash — use AT HOP card or contactless payment. $50/week fare cap.
Auckland Transport (AT)
Train
Western, Southern, Eastern, and Onehunga lines. Airport: AirportLink bus to Puhinui Station then train (45 min total).
Ferry
Waiheke Island (35 min from downtown, Fullers360). Devonport ferry for North Head. 20+ sailings daily.
Fullers360
Walking
CBD is walkable. Wynyard Quarter, Viaduct, Queen Street all on foot. Ponsonby ~20 min walk from CBD.
Transit Tip
Get an AT HOP card at the airport or any convenience store — it saves at least 20% on every fare and caps at $50/week.

Neighborhoods

CBD / City Centre

Urban Hub
The commercial heart with Queen Street shopping, Sky Tower, and most GF restaurants clustered here.

Wynyard Quarter

Waterfront
Revitalized harbourside precinct with Silo Park, seafood restaurants (Baduzzi, The Good Luck Coconut), and weekend markets.

Ponsonby

Trendy & Leafy
Auckland's cafe culture capital. Home to Little Bird Kitchen, Olas Arepas, and Mekong Baby. Boutique shopping on Ponsonby Rd.

Devonport

Village Charm
Charming harbourside village a short ferry ride from downtown. Historic North Head, galleries, and seaside walks.

Waiheke Island

Wine & Beaches
Auckland's island escape — boutique vineyards (Mudbrick), olive groves, golden beaches. 35-minute ferry from downtown.

Frequently Asked Questions

Auckland scores 8/10 on our GF friendliness scale, making it one of the easier cities for celiac travelers. New Zealand has strong awareness of coeliac disease, menus are widely labeled, and there are multiple 100% dedicated gluten-free restaurants in the city.
Top-rated 100% GF restaurants include Giapo (dedicated GF ice cream), HNT Kitchen (GF bowls), The Chip Shop (GF fish & chips), The GF Depot (GF cafe), Little Bird Kitchen (vegan/GF), The Attic Bar & Restaurant (Coeliac NZ accredited), and Olas Arepas (corn-based arepas).
Yes. Pak'nSave, New World, and Countdown/Woolworths all carry dedicated GF sections. Farro specializes in organic and GF products, and The Gluten Free Grocer in Mt Eden is a 100% gluten-free store. Look for the Crossed Grain logo — Coeliac New Zealand's official certification on 300+ products.

Travel Tips

  • Download the AT Mobile app for real-time bus/train/ferry tracking and AT HOP card top-ups.
  • Book Hobbiton and Waiheke wine tours at least a week ahead in summer — they sell out fast.
  • Auckland's UV index is extreme in summer — wear sunscreen even on cloudy days.
  • The best GF dining is concentrated in the CBD and Ponsonby — plan meals in those areas.
  • Waiheke Island deserves a full day — don't try to rush it as a half-day trip.
  • Mudbrick Vineyard has stunning views but book ahead, especially for weekend lunch.
What I'd Do Differently
  • I'd spend an extra day on Waiheke Island — one day wasn't enough to explore the vineyards and beaches.
  • I was really looking forward to Mt Eden but never actually made it there — next time for sure!
  • I'd add a day trip to Rotorua — the geothermal pools and Māori cultural experiences are only a few hours south and I wish I'd made the time.
