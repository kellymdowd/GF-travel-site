GF Friendliness

Manageable

India is a genuinely mixed bag for celiac travelers. FSSAI has formal gluten-free labeling standards on the books (the same 20ppm threshold used by the EU and FDA), but enforcement is largely self-declared and inconsistently followed — independent testing has found real GF-labeled products exceeding that threshold. Naturally gluten-free South Indian staples like dosa, idli, and rice-and-dal combinations make everyday eating easier than you'd expect, but hidden gluten in everyday ingredients like asafoetida (hing) and curry thickeners means vigilance is essential everywhere, and dedicated GF restaurants still cluster heavily in the bigger metros.

India scores 5/10 overall — Manageable rather than easy — and that average hides a real range: Hyderabad's upscale dining scene scored 8/10 with two fully dedicated GF restaurants, while Bengaluru and Goa each came in at 4/10, where celiac-aware options exist but take more research and clear communication with staff. There's no formal national GF certification body — the Celiac Society of India is an advocacy and support organization, not a labeling authority — so I verified ingredients restaurant by restaurant rather than looking for a seal. Naturally gluten-free staples like dosa, idli, and rice-and-dal dishes make everyday eating more manageable than the score alone suggests, but hidden gluten in ingredients like asafoetida (hing) and curry thickeners means it's worth staying alert everywhere, not just at unfamiliar restaurants.
I visited in April 2026, which is past the ideal window for two of these three cities — Bengaluru and Hyderabad are both better in the cooler months, roughly October through February, and even Goa, whose season runs later into April, was firmly in pre-monsoon heat by the time I got there. If your schedule is flexible, I'd aim for November through February across all three cities instead. April meant real heat management — electrolytes, sun protection, pacing outdoor time — that a cooler-month trip wouldn't require.
