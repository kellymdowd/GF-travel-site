Getting Around

Walking
Bergen center is compact and walkable. Most attractions within 15 minutes on foot.
Bybanen (Light Rail)
Single line from airport to city center. Main transit connection.
Fløibanen
Funicular to Mt. Fløyen. Runs year-round, ~8 min each way.
Ferries
Norled ferries to islands and fjord communities. Express boat to Flåm/Sognefjorden.
Transit Tip
Bergen's center is small enough to walk everywhere. Save the Bybanen for the airport transfer and the Fløibanen for the views.

Frequently Asked Questions

Bergen scores 7/10 for GF friendliness. Norwegian restaurants generally label allergens well, staff knowledge is high, and fresh seafood from the fish market is naturally gluten-free. Dedicated GF options like Peppes Pizza (NCF accredited) and Colonialen (celiac-owned) make it easier.
Bergen has 6 tested GF-friendly restaurants including Peppes Pizza (dedicated GF protocol) and Colonialen Brasseriet (celiac-owned). For groceries, try Meny at Galleriet, Life health store at Galleriet, or Coop Mega at Danmarksplass. The fish market offers naturally GF fresh seafood.
May through September offers the best weather for fjord cruises and hiking. Bergen averages 230 rainy days per year, so pack rain gear regardless of when you visit. September brings mild temperatures (8-15°C) and fewer crowds.

Travel Tips

  • Download the Skyss app for Bergen public transport
  • Buy Fløibanen tickets online — the queue at the base station can be very long in summer
  • The fish market is best in the morning when boats arrive with fresh catch
  • Bergen Card offers free/discounted museums and some transport — worth it for 2+ days
What I'd Do Differently
  • I'd spend an extra day for a fjord cruise to Flåm — the Norway in a Nutshell route from Bergen is spectacular and worth the full day.
