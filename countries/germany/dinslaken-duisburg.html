Key Phrases

Grocery Stores
  • EDEKA in Dinslaken — GF options in the free-from section
  • REWE in Duisburg centre — German chain with good GF selection
  • DM (drugstore) in Duisburg centre — stocks GF pasta, bread, and snacks
Dining Reality
Very limited GF restaurant options in Duisburg and Dinslaken. Pack your own GF meals and snacks. For dedicated GF dining, consider day-tripping to Düsseldorf (30 min by train) where Isabella Pâtisserie offers fully gluten-free baked goods and meals.

Landmarks & Must-See Spots

4 spots

Tiger & Turtle – Magic Mountain

Free
A walkable roller coaster sculpture perched on a former zinc smelter slag heap. You can walk the entire looping track on foot — except the actual loop. At night, LED lights illuminate the structure for a magical effect. Open 24/7, free admission.
Duisburg-Hüttenheim · Ehinger Str.
Went here

Landschaftspark Duisburg-Nord

Free
A 180-hectare industrial heritage park built around a decommissioned ironworks. Climb the blast furnace for panoramic views, dive in Europe’s deepest indoor diving centre, or explore climbing walls and gardens. LED lights illuminate the park Friday to Sunday nights. Free admission.
Duisburg-Meiderich · Emscherstr. 71

Getting Around

Stadtbahn U79
Light rail connecting Duisburg to Düsseldorf. Quick and reliable for reaching the neighbouring city for GF dining.
From €1.60
Tram 901/903
Trams cover central Duisburg. The 901 and 903 lines run through the main areas and connect to the Hauptbahnhof.
DVG (Duisburger Verkehrsgesellschaft)
Buses
Local bus network covers areas the trams don’t reach, including Dinslaken. Useful for getting between the two cities.
DVG & regional services
Regional Trains
RE and S-Bahn trains connect Duisburg to the wider Ruhr region and Düsseldorf in around 30 minutes. Duisburg Hbf is well-connected.
VRR network
Getting There
Landschaftspark is accessible by public transport from Duisburg Hbf. Tiger & Turtle is more remote — consider a taxi or a combination of tram and a short walk.

Travel Tips

  • Tiger & Turtle is best at sunset or after dark when the LED illumination transforms the sculpture. Time your visit accordingly.
  • Landschaftspark is huge — wear comfortable shoes and allow 2–3 hours to explore properly. The blast furnace climb alone takes a while.
  • Very limited GF dining in this area. Eat in Düsseldorf before or after your visit, or pack meals from the grocery store.
  • Both Tiger & Turtle and Landschaftspark are completely free — this makes for a great budget day trip from Düsseldorf or elsewhere in the Ruhr.
