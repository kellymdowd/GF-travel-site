Landmarks & Must-See Spots

9 spots

Römer

Historic
Frankfurt's medieval city hall and the heart of the Altstadt. The stepped-gable facade is one of Germany's most iconic cityscapes. The Römerberg square in front hosts the famous Christmas market.
Altstadt · Römerberg
Went here

Frankfurt Cathedral

Historic
Imperial cathedral where Holy Roman Emperors were once crowned. You can climb the dome for panoramic views over the Altstadt and the Main river. Free entry to the cathedral; small fee for the tower.
Altstadt · Domplatz
Went here

Iron Footbridge

Walk
Pedestrian bridge crossing the Main river, connecting the Altstadt to Sachsenhausen. Covered in love locks and offers some of the best skyline views of "Mainhattan" — especially at sunset.
River Main · Eiserner Steg

Kleinmarkthalle

Market
Frankfurt's beloved indoor food market. Over 150 stalls selling fresh produce, meats, cheeses, and international specialties. Great for browsing and picking up naturally GF snacks.
Altstadt · Hasengasse 5–7
Went here

Rententurm

Historic
A medieval tower from the 15th century, one of the last remaining parts of Frankfurt's old city fortifications. Sits near the Main riverfront in the Altstadt.
Altstadt · Mainkai
Went here

Frankfurter Römer

Historic
The broader Römerberg square and its surrounding half-timbered buildings, reconstructed to evoke medieval Frankfurt. A different angle from the Römer city hall — the full square is the real showpiece.
Altstadt · Römerberg
Went here

Domturm

Historic
The cathedral tower rising above Frankfurt's Altstadt skyline. Climb the narrow staircase for sweeping views over the old town rooftops, the Main river, and the modern skyline beyond.
Altstadt · Domplatz
Went here

Main Tower

Viewpoint
Frankfurt's only skyscraper with a public observation deck. 200 metres up with 360-degree views of the city, the Taunus mountains, and the Main river. Best at sunset.
Westend · Neue Mainzer Straße
Went here

Frankfurt Hauptbahnhof

Transport
One of Europe's busiest railway stations and an architectural landmark in its own right. The grand Neo-Renaissance facade is worth a look even if you're not catching a train.
Bahnhofsviertel · Am Hauptbahnhof

Neighborhoods

Altstadt

Historic & touristy
The reconstructed old town around Römerberg square. Home to the Cathedral, Kleinmarkthalle, and half-timbered houses. This is where you'll spend most of your sightseeing time — compact and very walkable.

Sachsenhausen

Local & lively
South of the Main river, reached by crossing the Iron Footbridge. Known for its traditional apple wine taverns and the Museum Embankment (Museumsufer). A more local feel than the tourist-heavy Altstadt.

Westend

Business & residential
Frankfurt's financial district with the skyline that earned it the "Mainhattan" nickname. Home to the Main Tower observation deck, the Alte Oper, and upscale dining. Where the hotel is located.

Getting Around

U-Bahn
Underground metro system covering the city centre and suburbs. Fast and frequent — your primary way to get around.
RMV network
S-Bahn
Regional commuter trains connecting the airport, Hauptbahnhof, and surrounding towns. S8/S9 from the airport takes 10–15 minutes.
Airport connection
Tram
Tram lines criss-cross the centre and reach areas the U-Bahn doesn't. Useful for getting to Sachsenhausen and the Museum Embankment.
Walking
Frankfurt's centre is very walkable. Altstadt to Sachsenhausen is a pleasant 15-minute stroll across the Iron Footbridge.
Local Tip
Get the Frankfurt Card for unlimited transport plus attraction discounts. Available as 1-day or 2-day passes from the tourist info at Hauptbahnhof or Römer.

Travel Tips

  • Frankfurt is very walkable in the centre. You can cover the Altstadt, Cathedral, Römer, and Kleinmarkthalle in a single morning on foot.
  • Kleinmarkthalle is great for browsing — pick up fresh fruit, cheese, or naturally GF snacks to take with you.
  • Cross the Iron Footbridge to Sachsenhausen for a different vibe — more local, less touristy, with traditional apple wine taverns.
  • Main Tower is best at sunset. Time your visit for golden hour and you'll see the city lights come on from 200 metres up.
  • The S8 or S9 from the airport to Hauptbahnhof takes only 10–15 minutes. No need for a taxi unless you have heavy luggage.
  • Steakhouses are your best bet for GF dining in Frankfurt. Block House and MAREDO both have reliable labeling systems.
What I'd Do Differently
  • I'd skip VAPIANO and spend that meal at HANS IM GLÜCK instead — the GF burger buns are actually good.
  • I'd time Main Tower for sunset rather than visiting during the day — the views are worth planning around.
