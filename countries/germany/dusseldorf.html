Walking
The city centre is very walkable. Königsallee, Altstadt, and the Rhine promenade are all within easy reach on foot.
Good to know
Doors on trams and trains don’t open automatically — press the green button to open them. The DüsseldorfCard (€12+) covers transit and includes museum discounts.

Travel Tips

  • Königsallee (“Kö”) is the main shopping street and the heart of the city. Even if you’re not shopping, the canal and architecture are worth a stroll.
  • Isabella Pâtisserie is pricey but worth every euro for safe, dedicated GF baked goods. Stock up for the road.
  • Book Vabali Spa in advance, especially on weekends. It’s popular with locals and can fill up.
  • The Christmas markets along the Kö are lovely in December — hot Glühwein is naturally GF, but be careful with food stalls.
  • The Altstadt (“Old Town”) is known as the “longest bar in the world” — hundreds of bars and restaurants packed into a few blocks. Many offer Altbier, the local dark beer (not GF).
  • Contactless payment is widely accepted, but some smaller shops and Christmas market vendors may prefer cash. Carry some euros.
What I'd Do Differently
  • I’d visit in warmer months to enjoy the Rhine promenade and outdoor cafés properly.
  • I’d spend a full day exploring the MedienHafen area with its Gehry buildings — we only passed through briefly.
