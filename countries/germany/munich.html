Landmarks & Must-See Spots

11 spots

Marienplatz

Historic
Munich's central square since 1158. Home to the New Town Hall with its famous Glockenspiel, which chimes daily. The heart of the Altstadt.
Altstadt

Frauenkirche

Historic
Gothic cathedral with iconic domed towers that define Munich's skyline. The south tower offers panoramic views. Look for the “Devil's Footprint” legend inside.
Altstadt

Viktualienmarkt

Market
Munich's famous daily food market since 1807. Fresh produce, Bavarian specialties, flowers, and a beer garden. Great for naturally GF snacks.
Altstadt · Near Marienplatz

Hofbräuhaus München

Beer Hall
The world's most famous beer hall, founded in 1589. A must-visit for the atmosphere — live oompah music, giant steins, Bavarian culture. GF options are very limited.
Altstadt · Platzl

English Garden

Nature
One of the world's largest urban parks. Beer garden at the Chinese Tower, river surfing at the Eisbach wave, and sprawling meadows. Worth the trek north.
North of Altstadt

Karlsplatz (Stachus)

Historic
Major square at the western edge of the Altstadt, with the Karlstor medieval gate framing the entrance to the pedestrian zone.
West Altstadt

Odeonsplatz

Historic
Grand square flanked by the Feldherrnhalle and Theatine Church. A hub for Bavarian history and architecture at the south end of Ludwigstraße.
North Altstadt

St. Peter's Church

Historic
Munich's oldest parish church. The tower (299 steps) offers the best panoramic city views. Known locally as “Alter Peter.”
Altstadt · Near Marienplatz

Viktualienmarkt Beergarden

Beer Garden
Beer garden in the middle of the market — you can bring your own food from the stalls. Classic Munich experience under the chestnut trees.
Altstadt · Viktualienmarkt

Technische Universität München

Historic
One of Europe's top technical universities with a beautiful campus. The main building on Arcisstraße is architecturally impressive.
Schwabing

Stachus Passagen

Shopping
Underground shopping center beneath Karlsplatz. Useful for supplies and a warm escape from winter weather on the walk to Marienplatz.
West Altstadt · Underground

Neighborhoods

Altstadt

Historic & central
Munich's medieval old town, centered on Marienplatz. Home to most landmarks, Viktualienmarkt, Hofbräuhaus, and the pedestrian shopping zone. Walkable, lively, and where you'll spend most of your time.

Schwabing

University & bohemian
Munich's intellectual and artistic quarter, north of the Altstadt. Home to the university, independent cafes, bookshops, and the English Garden. Where echt jetzt GF bakery is located.

Neuhausen

Residential & relaxed
A quieter residential neighborhood west of the centre with tree-lined streets, local beer gardens, and the Nymphenburg Palace nearby. Where the Roomers Hotel is located.

Travel Tips

  • Visit Hofbräuhaus for the atmosphere and a beer, but eat elsewhere for GF safety. The food is heavily wheat-based.
  • Christmas markets have very limited GF food — eat before going and enjoy the Glühwein (mulled wine, naturally GF) instead.
  • The English Garden is worth the trek north. The Chinese Tower beer garden is a classic Munich spot, even in winter.
  • Cash is still preferred at some spots in Munich, particularly smaller cafes and market stalls. Carry some euros.
  • Most GF beer is available at REWE or EDEKA. German beer halls rarely stock GF options.
  • Isabella Glutenfreie Pâtisserie (Hackenstraße, near Viktualienmarkt) is a hidden gem — entirely GF and worth a detour.
What I'd Do Differently
  • I'd visit echt jetzt bakery for GF pretzels — the one thing I missed that every celiac raves about.
  • I'd pack more snacks for Christmas market days. There's almost nothing GF at the stalls and you get hungry fast in the cold.
