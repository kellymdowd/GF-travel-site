Landmarks & Must-See Spots

4 spots

Cologne Cathedral

Historic
Germany's most visited landmark and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. This iconic Gothic cathedral took over 600 years to complete. Free to enter — the twin spires dominate the skyline from every angle.
Domkloster · next to Hauptbahnhof
Went here

Hohenzollernbrücke

Walk
The famous love lock bridge spanning the Rhine. Walk across for some of the best views of the Cathedral and the Old Town. Thousands of padlocks cover the railings — a Cologne tradition.
Rhine · Cathedral to Deutz
Went here

Old Market (Alter Markt)

Historic
The historic heart of Cologne's Altstadt. Colorful buildings line the square, which hosts markets and is surrounded by traditional brewhouses serving Kölsch beer.
Altstadt · Old Town
Went here

Weihnachtsmarkt auf dem Neumarkt

Market
One of Cologne's beloved Christmas markets, set around the Neumarkt square. Glühwein, handcrafts, and festive atmosphere — but very limited GF food options. Eat beforehand.
Neumarkt · City Centre
Went here

Getting Around

Walking
Most major sights are within walking distance of the Cathedral. The Old Town, Rhine promenade, and Neumarkt are all easily connected on foot.
Tram & U-Bahn
Cologne's KVB network covers the city well. Trams and underground trains run frequently and connect all major areas.
KVB network
Bus
Supplements the tram network. Useful for reaching areas not served by rail. Part of the KVB system, same tickets.
24h service weekends
KölnCard
Unlimited transit plus museum discounts. €9/24h single, €19/24h group. Worth it if you're doing multiple attractions beyond walking distance.
€9 single / €19 group
Local Tip
Most sights are walkable from the Cathedral. The KölnCard is good value if you're doing multiple attractions, but for a short visit you can easily get by on foot.

Travel Tips

  • Cologne Cathedral is free to enter. The tower climb and guided tours are paid, but you can wander the nave for free anytime.
  • Walk across the Hohenzollernbrücke for the best photo angles of the Cathedral and Rhine skyline.
  • Christmas markets are magical but have almost no GF food. Eat at a restaurant beforehand, then enjoy the atmosphere and Glühwein (most is naturally GF, but always confirm).
  • Visit the Cologne Triangle observation deck in late afternoon — the sunset light over the Cathedral is stunning and the €3 admission is a bargain.
  • Kölsch beer (served in tiny 0.2L glasses) is the local tradition. Not GF, but the ritual of the Köbes (waiters) constantly refilling your glass is worth observing.
What I'd Do Differently
  • I'd pack more GF snacks — the markets are incredible but being unable to eat anything there gets old fast.
  • I'd take the Rhine cruise — the Cathedral views from the water looked spectacular and we ran out of time.
