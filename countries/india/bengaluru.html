Gluten-Free Survival Guide

Key Phrases
"ನನಗೆ ಗ್ಲುಟನ್ ಅಲರ್ಜಿ ಇದೆ" (naanu gluten allergy ide) I have a gluten allergy (celiac disease)
"ಗೋಧಿ ಹಿಟ್ಟು ಬೇಡ" (godhi hittu beda) No wheat flour, please
Bengaluru is a major tech hub — English is widely understood by hotel and restaurant staff, so a Kannada card is a nice-to-have rather than a necessity. It's still worth carrying a printed English + Kannada card stating "I have celiac disease and cannot eat wheat, barley, oats, or rye" for street vendors.
Grocery Stores
  • Nature's Basket — Koramangala, Indiranagar, Sadashivnagar
  • Foodhall — 1MG Mall, VR Mall Mahadevpura
  • Both stock GF bread, pasta, and millets in a "healthy alternatives" section
Safe Cuisines
  • Idli — steamed, naturally GF, and the lower-risk choice of the three
  • Dosa / uttapam — naturally GF if made on a dedicated griddle; specify "rice dosa only, no rava, no shared oil"
  • Rice-based curries and dal — ask about wheat used as a thickener before ordering
Certification to Look For
India has no formal national gluten-free certification or labeling body comparable to the FDA in the US or FACE in Spain. The Celiac Society of India exists as an advocacy and support organization but doesn't run a labeling program — don't expect certified "gluten-free" labeling on packaged goods the way you might elsewhere.

Getting Around

Namma Metro
The fastest way to move around the city, with the Purple and Green lines covering many key areas and avoiding Bengaluru's notorious traffic.
Best for: cross-city trips
Auto-Rickshaws
Metered autos exist, but fares are often disputed. Book through an app instead for fixed, upfront pricing.
Best for: short local hops
BMTC Buses
Public buses cover the areas the metro doesn't reach — extensive but can be slow given traffic and limited English signage.
Best for: budget travel
Ride-Hailing
Uber and Ola cabs are widely available across the city and a comfortable option for longer or evening trips.
Best for: door-to-door comfort
Local Tip
Skip street-hailed autos — use Namma Yatri, Ola Auto, or Uber Auto instead for fixed, upfront pricing with no haggling.

Travel Tips

  • Bengaluru's GF dining scene is thin relative to its size — research restaurants ahead of time rather than expecting to find safe options on the fly.
  • Hotel breakfast buffets carry real cross-contact risk from shared toasters and flour dust in the air — ask the kitchen for made-to-order eggs or dosa instead of self-serve items.
  • Nature's Basket and Foodhall are your grocery safety net for GF bread, pasta, and millets if a meal out doesn't pan out.
  • Skip street-hailed autos — Namma Yatri, Ola Auto, and Uber Auto give upfront pricing and are far more reliable for getting around.
