# GF Gone Global — Restaurant GF Scoring Rubric (Kelly's, "Rubric 3")

_Saved by Moose 2026-10-25 from Kelly's own text. This is the authoritative scoring system for all guides, carousels, captions, and the Score Explainer card. Score restaurants by counting categories — never by vibe._

## Restaurant-level GF score (out of 5)
Five categories, each scored 0 or 1. The total is the score — it tells a celiac traveler how confidently they can eat at that specific restaurant.

1. **GF Options Available** — Does the restaurant have GF items you can actually order? 0 = no clear GF options / 1 = yes, GF items available
2. **Menu Labeling** — Are GF items marked on the menu, or does staff proactively guide you? 0 = not marked, have to ask / 1 = clearly labeled or staff-guided
3. **Good Range of Options** — More than 1–2 dishes? Real choices? 0 = very limited / 1 = multiple options
4. **Safety Awareness** — Dedicated GF facility, mentions cross-contamination protocols, or staff understand celiac needs? 0 = no clear safety awareness / 1 = dedicated GF or documented safety protocols
5. **Would Recommend to a Celiac Friend** — Overall gut feeling — would you send someone there without worry? 0 = proceed with caution / 1 = would recommend

**100% gluten-free restaurants automatically score 5/5** — if a restaurant is entirely dedicated to gluten-free cooking, every category is met by definition.

**5/5 does NOT require a dedicated restaurant.** A non-dedicated restaurant earns 5/5 by meeting all five categories (e.g. labeled GF menu + range + safety protocols + recommend). Do not describe 5/5 as "reserved for dedicated" in copy — Kelly corrected this 2026-10-25.

## Verdict scale
- **5 — Excellent:** eat with total confidence
- **4 — Great:** strong GF awareness
- **3 — Good:** solid options with some communication needed
- **2 — Fair:** possible but limited
- **1 — Caution:** very few confirmed safe options

## Note on scoring (Kelly's, published with the rubric)
Scores are based on personal experience and research, and they err on the generous side. A score reflects what a celiac traveler can reasonably expect, not a worst-case scenario. When in doubt, always communicate your needs directly with the restaurant.
